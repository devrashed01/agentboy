How it works

Out of the way until it matters.

AgentBoy lives in your menu bar. It shows just enough at rest, opens a little when you point at it, and becomes a full panel when you click.

01 · At rest

A glance at the top of your screen

Session and weekly usage, plus how many agents are working, thinking, waiting or idle.

02 · Hover

The one thing that matters

A question waiting on you, a plan to review, or simply how your agents are doing.

03 · Click

Every agent, in one panel

What each session is doing right now, what it asked, and what finished today.

Questions and permissions

Answer your agents without leaving what you're doing.

When Claude asks a multiple-choice question or wants permission to run something, the pill peeks with the question, and you answer in one click.

  • Every option Claude Code offers, word for word. Nothing cropped, nothing dropped.
  • Skip, like pressing Esc in the terminal, or hand it back to the terminal.
  • A soft chime when a question arrives (or not, with Quiet mode).
Done means done

Know the moment a long task finishes.

Start something big, go do something else. When an agent finishes, the pill tells you what it did. It stays quiet if you're already looking at that session.

  • Pick when to hear about it: any task, or only ones over 1 or 5 minutes.
  • Go to Session jumps straight to its terminal, editor or the Claude app.
  • Today's activity keeps a quiet log of what finished and what you answered.
Usage, honestly

Your limits, with the reset times that make them useful.

Session and weekly usage, straight from Claude Code, with when each one resets and a trend line. You get a heads-up at 80% and 95%.

  • If the numbers are old, it says how old instead of guessing.
  • After a reset it shows a dash until fresh numbers arrive, never a made-up 0%.

One shortcut

⌃ ⌥ Space opens the panel from anywhere, right on the first waiting question.

Quiet mode

For an hour, until tomorrow, or until you turn it off. No sounds or pop-ups; it still counts what's waiting.

Every display

A pill on each screen, so it's always where you're looking. Only the one you use grows.

Make it yours

Graphite, Black, Navy or System theme. Tune the pill's padding, size and how far below the menu bar it sits.

Updates itself

New versions install from inside the app, in a click. No re-downloading.

Smooth, and light

Every transition is spring-animated and tested frame by frame. At rest it uses well under 1% CPU.

Private by design

Everything stays on your Mac.

AgentBoy reads what Claude Code already tells it, on your machine. No account, no analytics, no servers of ours.

  • ✓Connect adds hooks to ~/.claude/settings.json and wraps your status line so usage can be read. Your own hooks and status line keep working exactly as before.
  • ✓A backup of settings.json is saved before every change.
  • ✓Disconnect restores the original, in one click.
  • ✓The only network call is checking GitHub for updates.
Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Download

    Get the latest AgentBoy.dmg from the button below.

  2. Drag to Applications

    Open the disk image and drag AgentBoy into Applications.

  3. Open it once

    Right-click AgentBoy, choose Open, then Open again. Only needed the first time.

  4. Connect

    Click the pill, open Settings (the magic wand) and click Connect next to Claude Code.

Why the extra step? AgentBoy isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks before the first launch. You can also try opening it once, then click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security. Updates after that install from inside the app, with no prompts.

Questions

Good to know.

What do I need?

macOS 14 or later and Claude Code installed (the claude command). Usage numbers appear for Claude Pro and Max accounts, because that's when Claude Code reports them.

Is it free?

Yes. Free to download and use.

Which questions can I answer from the pill?

Claude's multiple-choice questions and permission prompts, with every option it offers. Plans and questions Claude asks in plain text are shown so you don't miss them, and you reply in the terminal.

Does it work with several sessions at once?

That's what it's for. Every running session shows up, each with its own status and activity, and questions from all of them line up in one place.

How do I remove it?

Click Disconnect in Settings → Claude Code to restore your original settings, then quit the app and move it to the Trash.
